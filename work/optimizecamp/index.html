OptimizeCamp

An AI visibility platform. It tracks how ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Claude mention and cite a brand, scores the result, and turns each gap into a fix or a content draft. It works for any website; WordPress sites can also sync content and publish fixes back.

It runs a set of buyer prompts against each AI engine on a schedule and records mentions, citations, position and sentiment for the brand and its competitors, then rolls them into a Visibility Score and alerts when a citation is lost.

Gaps become a prioritized fix queue and content briefs. Content comes in from a URL or pasted text. WordPress gets one-click sync and publish-back, and drafts always wait for a person to publish them.

Free tools: AI Visibility Checker, AI Crawler Checker and an llms.txt generator.
